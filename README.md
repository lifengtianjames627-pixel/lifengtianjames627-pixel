1) Simulation

- ASE: used for xyz coordinate resolution & chemical environment analysis in molecules/solids

- ATAT & ICEt: used for special quasirandom structure (SQS) generation and cluser expansion (CE), especially for high-entropy alloy (HEA) systems

- Avogadro + ORCA/PySCF + Multiwfn + IQMOL: molecular-level electronic structure analysis & visualization 
  
- VESTA + LAMMPS + MDanalysis + VMD: MD simulation with classical & machine-learned potentials on solid systems

- VESTA + QuantumEspresso + BURAI: solid-level electronic structure analysis & visualization

2) Informatics

- pymatgen/matminer: material descriptors construction & arithmetics

- COD/CSD/MP/JARVIS: datasets for solid-state material information
